The scale

Why the phone, and not just a late night?

The light

Screens delay the body clock and hold back melatonin, the hormone that makes us sleepy. We take longer to drop off and wake up less alert. (Chang et al., 2015)

The pull

One reply. One video. One more. Night mode dims the screen. It does nothing about the conversation.

The cause

There is a name for this.

Bedtime procrastination: going to bed later than we meant to, with nothing outside of us forcing it.

Naming it helped me. It made the problem a habit instead of a character.

The name

The gap between two versions of me

My ideal self protects her health. My real self answers messages until two in the morning.

Carl Rogers called that distance incongruence.

I call it the small disappointment I feel every night before I sleep.

The self

On tired days, I believe the wrong one.

I'm lazy.

I slept four hours.

Mistaking a temporary state for a permanent trait is how a habit quietly becomes an identity.

The self

Can I trust what I think my sleep is like?

In one survey, 97 percent of people rated their own leadership as average or better. Ninety-seven percent cannot be above average.

We rate ourselves kindly. So I think I sleep about seven hours is a guess, and a flattering one.

That is why the journal asks for times, not opinions.

The self

So I am building something small.

A sleep journal that asks three things:

  1. What time you meant to sleep
  2. What time you actually slept, and what kept you awake
  3. How you felt the next morning

After a week, it shows you your own gap. Not a lecture. Your own evidence.

Three weeks, three different reasons: worrying, an unsettled schedule, scrolling. On twenty of twenty-one nights, someone slept later than they meant to.

I assumed phones would lead. For one of us they did. The gap was the only thing all three of us shared.

The findings

A target I can actually hit

My first instinct was “sleep eight hours.” On a shift that ends at 12:30, that was never a plan. It was a wish.

  • In bed by 1:30, not whenever
  • Phone charging across the room
  • Replies can wait until morning

Small, specific, and tied to a time. A goal without one is just a thing I would like.

The change
